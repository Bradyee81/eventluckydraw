**What's new**

- **Buzzer setup checks the whole connection.** After you press Connect in Setup → Config → Buzzer setup, the app now waits until the buzzer actually reaches it, then shows "Buzzer is connected and talking to the app". If the buzzer joined WiFi but the app can't hear it, you get a clear reason and fix, such as the firewall, a Public network, or another copy of Lucky Draw running.
- **One-click firewall fix.** A new **Allow buzzer through firewall** button (one admin prompt) stops Windows blocking the buzzer on new venue routers.
- **More reliable laptop address.** The buzzer is now given the laptop's WiFi address specifically, so a cable, hotspot or VPN on the laptop can't send it to the wrong place.
- **Clearer status.** Buzzer setup shows the WiFi network, the laptop's address and the firewall status at a glance, and mentions 2.4 GHz if the buzzer can't join the WiFi.

**New install:** download the file ending in `_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. Already have Lucky Draw 0.2.0 or later? Open it and press **Update now**.
